Employee Leave Policy

Annual Leave:
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Sick Leave:
Employees receive 12 days of sick leave per year. A medical certificate may be required for sick leave exceeding three consecutive working days.

Casual Leave:
Employees are entitled to 8 days of casual leave per year.

Public Holidays:
Employees receive paid leave on officially recognized company holidays. The holiday calendar is published by the HR department at the beginning of each year.

Maternity Leave:
Eligible female employees may receive maternity leave according to applicable Indian labor laws and company policy.

Paternity Leave:
Eligible employees may receive 10 working days of paternity leave following the birth or adoption of a child.

Leave Approval:
Employees should submit leave requests through the HR portal at least three working days before the planned leave whenever possible.

Emergency Leave:
In emergencies, employees should notify their reporting manager as soon as reasonably possible and submit the formal leave request afterward.

Leave Carry Forward:
Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the following year up to a maximum of 10 days.

Leave Encashment:
Employees may request encashment of eligible unused annual leave according to company policy and applicable rules.

Unapproved Absence:
Absence without approval or proper notification may be treated as unauthorized absence and may result in disciplinary action.
