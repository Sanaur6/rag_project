Company Information

Company Name: TechNova Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

TechNova Solutions is a software development company specializing in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, web applications, and data engineering.

The company was founded in 2021 and its headquarters are located in Bengaluru, Karnataka.

The company currently has approximately 120 employees.

Working Hours:
Employees generally work from Monday to Friday, from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. Employees are expected to maintain a minimum of 8 working hours per day.

Remote Work:
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with approval from their reporting manager. Remote work is not allowed during critical client meetings unless specifically approved.

Employee Benefits:
Full-time employees receive health insurance, paid annual leave, sick leave, and access to professional development programs.

Learning and Development:
Employees can request reimbursement for approved technical courses, certifications, and conferences. The maximum annual reimbursement is 25,000 INR per employee.

Performance Reviews:
Employee performance reviews are conducted twice a year, generally in June and December.

Probation:
New employees normally have a six-month probation period. During probation, employees are eligible for basic company benefits but some additional benefits may begin only after confirmation.

Notice Period:
The standard notice period for confirmed employees is 60 days unless a different period is specified in the employment contract.
